جهيمان — القيامة بين الركن والمقام

Juhayman — The Resurrection Between the Rukn and the Maqam
Status: ForthcomingGenre: Historical religious novelBy: Ahmed Alhafiz — أحمد الحافظ

“Juhayman — The Resurrection Between the Rukn and the Maqam” is a forthcoming historical religious novel by Ahmed Alhafiz, rooted in the seizure of the Grand Mosque in Mecca on 20 November 1979, when Muhammad bin Abdullah al-Qahtani was proclaimed the Mahdi and a siege began that left a lasting mark on modern memory.

Rather than merely retelling the event, the novel examines the intellectual and psychological path by which religious zeal and confidence in sacred texts can become closed obedience and interpretation that no longer accepts correction. Its concern is not to mock faith, but to ask when a person begins to load a text with meanings it did not state and then treats those additions as part of the text itself.

The work combines history, religious debate, and philosophical questioning to explore mechanisms of extremism, group loyalty, pledge, and certainty. The novel is forthcoming by Ahmed Alhafiz.
