جُهَيْمَان — خوارج بين الركن والمقام

Juhayman — Kharijites Between the Rukn and the Maqam

A historical religious novel rooted in the seizure of the Grand Mosque in Mecca on 20 November 1979 and concerned with the path by which religious zeal can become closed obedience and interpretation that no longer accepts correction.

Status: forthcomingGenre: historical religious novelAuthor: Ahmed Alhafiz

About the work

History as a question about interpretation and certainty

The novel does not merely retell the Grand Mosque seizure. It examines the intellectual and psychological route by which a person can add desired meanings to a sacred text and then treat those additions as though they were part of the text itself.

History, religious debate, and philosophical inquiry meet inside the experience of the characters. The central concern is not mockery of faith, but the collapse of correction when group loyalty, pledge, and expectation of salvation become stronger than review.
