Umm Abbas — Bringing Back the Beloved and Returning the Divorced Woman
Status: ForthcomingGenre: Psychological horror novelBy: Ahmed Alhafiz — أحمد الحافظ

“Umm Abbas — Bringing Back the Beloved and Returning the Divorced Woman” is a psychological horror novel that begins with something small: a mysterious paper, a frightened household, and a family trying to decide whether what is happening to their daughter is illness, delusion, or something they cannot explain.

The novel moves through the uncertain territory between fear, belief, medicine, and religious interpretation. Rather than offering the reader an easy answer, it keeps several explanations alive at once. The body becomes a place of doubt, and the people closest to the protagonist become part of the fear because each person reads the same signs through what they believe or dread.

Its horror is built less on sudden shock than on the erosion of trust inside a home and on a persistent question: what remains of a person when they are no longer certain that their own actions belong to them? The novel is forthcoming by Ahmed Alhafiz.
