About the work

A public description without exposing the manuscript

“Umm Abbas” begins with a predicament rather than an answer: what happens when a family member reports a frightening experience that offers no decisive proof, while medicine, inherited beliefs, fear, and shame compete to explain it?

The novel is not presented here as a medical report or proof of supernatural causation. It is fiction using ambiguity, family dynamics, the body, and language to construct psychological horror. Linked research therefore verifies medical and social phenomena outside the manuscript rather than treating fiction as evidence.

Publication transparency
