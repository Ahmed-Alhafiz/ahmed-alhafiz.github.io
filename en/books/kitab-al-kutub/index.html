Kitab al-Kutub — The Book of Books

About the work

Who shapes memory when power and writing compete?

The novel follows Siraj al-Warraq through a historical landscape in which Baghdad and other cities become settings for questions about power, betrayal, and record-making. History is not decorative background; it is a field in which the relationship between what happened, what was written, and what authority allowed to survive is examined.

The central question is not only how an enemy reaches a city’s gates, but how trust can erode beforehand and how an official or fabricated account can outlive the event that produced it. The historical story therefore meets a philosophical inquiry into the responsibility of the writer and the force of the written word.
