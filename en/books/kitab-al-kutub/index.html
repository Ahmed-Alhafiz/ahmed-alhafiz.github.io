Kitab al-Kutub — The Book of Books
Status: ForthcomingGenre: Historical philosophical novelBy: Ahmed Alhafiz — أحمد الحافظ

“Kitab al-Kutub — The Book of Books” is a forthcoming historical philosophical novel by Ahmed Alhafiz that looks at the fall of cities not as an isolated military event, but as the result of a longer process of internal decay, fear, corruption, and the manipulation of memory.

The novel follows Siraj al-Warraq through a historical landscape in which Baghdad and other cities become settings for questions about power, writing, betrayal, and the authority of those who record events. History is not used as decoration; it becomes a field in which the relationship between the written word, political power, and collective memory is examined.

At its core, the novel asks how societies can be defeated before an enemy reaches their gates, and how an official or falsified account can outlive the violence that produced it. The book is forthcoming by Ahmed Alhafiz.
