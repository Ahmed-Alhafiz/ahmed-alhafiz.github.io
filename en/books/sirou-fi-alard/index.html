سيروا في الأرض

Sirou fi al-Ard — Travel Through the Earth
Status: ForthcomingGenre: Religious and intellectual nonfictionBy: Ahmed Alhafiz — أحمد الحافظ

“Sirou fi al-Ard — Travel Through the Earth” is a religious and intellectual work that begins with questions about the origin of creation and the relationship between revelation and science. It reads the Qur’anic call to travel and observe as a method of reflection and inquiry rather than as a decorative phrase.

The book addresses the tension that appears when scientific questions become a source of fear for some believers, or when religion is presented by some advocates of science as an opponent of reason. Instead of constructing a forced conflict, it tries to separate the questions: what does the text actually say, what does science actually establish, and where does human interpretation begin in each domain?

Its aim is not to settle every debate at once, but to encourage a calmer relationship between faith and knowledge in which inquiry is legitimate, observation is responsible, and science remains aware of its own limits. The book is forthcoming by Ahmed Alhafiz.
