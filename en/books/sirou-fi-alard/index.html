About the work

A public description without exposing the manuscript

“Qul Siru fi al-Ard fa-Unzuru Kayfa Bada’a al-Khalq” begins with the Qur’anic command to travel, observe, and ask how creation began. Its central concern is methodological: how can revelation, science, and human interpretation be kept in the right relation without making science an enemy of faith or turning a verse into a compressed scientific theory?

The current manuscript is publicly described only at the level of four broad tracks: ratq, fatq, and cosmic time; the human being taught names, language, and writing; the patterns of civilisation; and the use of matter and life. This page publishes no chapter-length excerpts or unstable publication data.

Publication transparency

The work has not yet been officially published. This page carries no ISBN, publisher, fixed date, reviews, or sales links. Those fields will be added only after public verification.
