Publication model

Three labels with different evidentiary weight

01 / DOSSIER

Research dossier

Extended argument, claim inventory, evidence appendix, annotated sources, revision history, and a citable version.

02 / BRIEF

Evidence brief

A narrower synthesis for a defined question. It is useful, but not presented as a complete field review.

03 / ESSAY

Critical essay

Interpretive literary or cultural analysis. Sources may inform it, but argument and reading remain the author’s.
