Research formats

Different formats for different questions

01 / DOSSIER

Research dossier

Extended argument, claim inventory, source trail, limitations, revision state, and portable citation data.

02 / BRIEF

Evidence brief

A narrower synthesis for a defined question, without presenting itself as a complete field review.

03 / ESSAY

Critical essay

Interpretive literary or cultural analysis in which the author’s argument remains visibly distinct from sourced factual claims.
