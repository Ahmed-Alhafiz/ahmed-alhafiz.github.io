Claim and evidence appendix

This page does not retell the essay. It exposes each inferential step: what is claimed, what kind of evidence supports it, how confident the dossier is, and what the source cannot establish.
How to read the labels

“High” means the selected sources strongly support a bounded claim, not that the sentence is infallible. “Medium” marks an integrative judgment on which informed readers may differ. No independent specialist review is complete.

C01 — What the verse says

Claim: Qur’an 21:30 states ratq followed by fatq attributed to God, but does not mention Big Bang terminology or a quantitative model.

Evidence: The primary text and classical lexical/exegetical discussion.

Confidence: High

Boundary: Divine attribution is the Qur’anic claim, not an empirical result produced by cosmology.

C02 — Classical interpretation

Claim: Al-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, and Ibn Kathir preserve multiple readings: joining/separation, differentiation of heavens and earths, and rain/vegetation being withheld then released.

Evidence: The exegetical texts themselves.

Confidence: High

Counterpoint: An exegete may prefer one reading. Preference does not erase historical plurality.

C03 — What the Big Bang is

Claim: The model is not a bomb expanding from a central point into external emptiness. It describes an expanding cosmic scale and an earlier hot, dense state.

Evidence: Official NASA explanation and modern cosmological modelling.

Confidence: High

Boundary: Global topology and the earliest quantum-gravity regime remain model-dependent.

C04 — Convergent evidence

Claim: Expansion, the CMB, primordial nucleosynthesis, and large-scale structure provide independent and mutually constraining support.

Evidence: Original expansion and CMB papers, COBE/FIRAS, Planck, PDG syntheses, and DESI.

Confidence: High

Boundary: Each line still involves calibration, inference, and explicit model assumptions.

C05 — Limits of empirical scope

Claim: The model alone does not establish creation from absolute nothing, divine agency, purpose, or a complete absolute first instant.

Evidence: Scope analysis of what the scientific sources model and measure.

Confidence: High

Boundary: Failure to empirically establish a metaphysical proposition is not proof of its negation.

C06 — The comparison judgment

Claim: A broad comparison between ratq-to-fatq and hot expanding cosmic history is possible; technical identity and exclusive exegesis are not demonstrated.

Evidence: An integration of text, interpretive plurality, and scientific description.

Confidence: Medium

Why medium: This is the author’s methodological judgment, not a sentence found in one source.

C07 — Revision without collapse

Claim: Planck and DESI display a highly successful standard framework alongside open tensions and possible revision in some components, especially dark energy.

Evidence: Final Planck analyses, DESI DR2 publication guides, and the July 2026 Lyman-alpha update.

Confidence: High

Boundary: Evolving-dark-energy hints are not a settled replacement for ΛCDM.

C08 — Relationship to the book

Claim: The forthcoming book generated the editorial question but does not function as evidence for public cosmological claims.

Evidence: The site’s disclosed editorial policy and link structure.

Confidence: High

How to challenge or correct a claim

  1. Identify the claim ID rather than objecting to the page as a whole.
  2. Open the cited source and test whether it supports that level of specificity.
  3. State whether the dispute concerns wording, attribution, data interpretation, or integration.
  4. Provide a stronger primary or specialist source and explain how it changes the claim or confidence.

A valid correction may narrow a claim or reduce confidence. Agreement with the final interpretation is not required; layer discipline is.
