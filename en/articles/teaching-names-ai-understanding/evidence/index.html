Claim and Evidence Register

The strength of this dossier does not depend on being “for” or “against” machine understanding. It depends on separating what an experiment measured from what was inferred, what remains contested, and what the author proposes as an analytical tool rather than validated science. This register maps 12 claims to 21 sources and makes every boundary visible.

How to read the register

Documented means the bounded proposition closely follows a primary source or original experiment. Documented with inference combines a published result with an explicitly labelled inferential boundary. Contested marks sources that differ in construct, method, or conclusion. Author synthesis marks a new organising framework that remains open to criticism and is not a validated benchmark. Confidence concerns the claim–evidence relationship; it is not a final verdict on every model or on consciousness.

Reciprocal audit

The machine-readable claims.json records the evidence IDs supporting each claim, while each evidentiary source declares the claims it supports. S09 and S14 are explicitly retained as comparative baselines rather than direct support for a central proposition; their presence is not used to inflate confidence.

Register limitations

  • A result on one model or task is not a verdict on all artificial intelligence or future architectures.
  • A probe that can decode information does not necessarily show that the information caused the answer.
  • Vision or robotic action adds functional grounding but does not demonstrate subjective sensory experience.
  • A philosophical position paper is not an experiment; an experiment cannot by itself settle a differently defined philosophical construct.
  • System self-report neither establishes nor disproves consciousness.
  • The related forthcoming book is the thematic origin of the question, not technical evidence.
  • The matrix and protocol are open analytical proposals without independent validation.

Review status

Author and source review remains in progress, and no independent NLP or philosophy-of-AI specialist has reviewed this edition. This appendix must not be represented as peer-reviewed research or used to declare a particular system conscious or non-conscious.
