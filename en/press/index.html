Extended biography

Ahmed Alhafiz is an Arabic writer and author whose forthcoming work spans historical and religious fiction, psychological horror, and intellectual nonfiction. His official website brings together book pages and independent research on religion and science, history and civilisation, fear and authority, and the philosophy of knowledge. The site keeps literary work distinct from public research and does not treat unpublished manuscripts as evidence for factual claims.
