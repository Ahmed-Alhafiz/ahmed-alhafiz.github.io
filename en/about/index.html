The writing project

Narrative tests the human situation; research tests the claim

Ahmed Alhafiz’s forthcoming work spans historical and religious fiction, psychological horror, and intellectual nonfiction. The common ground is the unstable border between a text and its interpretation, an event and its memory, fear and its cause, and evidence and the conclusions built upon it.

The research desk is therefore not extended advertising for unpublished books. Each dossier begins with an independent question, relies on evidence outside the manuscript, and states its review status and limits. When a question grows from a forthcoming work, that relationship is disclosed without treating the book as evidence for its own claims.
