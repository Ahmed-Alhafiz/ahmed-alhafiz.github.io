Ahmed Alhafiz — أحمد الحافظ

Writer and author

Ahmed Alhafiz — أحمد الحافظ is a writer and author whose work engages literature, thought, history, and religious questions. His writing combines narrative, research, and reflection, with particular attention to questions that emerge where individual experience meets history, faith, power, and memory.

His writing project does not treat fiction as separate from knowledge, or intellectual writing as an abstract exercise. The central aim is to build texts around clear questions and to use story, argument, and historical context to let readers examine an issue from more than one angle without turning the work into a slogan or a lecture.

His current works range across historical and philosophical fiction, psychological horror, and religious-intellectual nonfiction. This website serves as the official reference point for his author identity, books, and verified public links.
