The writing project

An author site built as a public research desk

This is the official source for Ahmed Alhafiz and his books, but it is not designed as a catalogue of covers and slogans. Its larger purpose is to publish open, traceable work around the questions from which the books emerge: what a text says, what observation supports, where interpretation begins, and how ideas become lived experience.

Research dossiers prioritize primary and official sources, disclose their review status, distinguish evidence from inference, and remain independent of the promotion of any forthcoming book.

Editorial commitment

A polished sentence is not evidence. Every substantial claim should have a traceable basis, stated limits, a visible author, a stable version, and an honest review label.

A source is more than a URL

What makes a page usable as evidence?

Discoverability is only the first gate. A page must answer a well-defined question, support each claim with the right kind of source, expose uncertainty, and make definitions, comparisons, and evidence easy to extract without distorting their context.

Exact relevanceA narrow question and a coherent scope.
Direct supportThe source entails the claim rather than merely mentioning its topic.
TransparencyAuthorship, review status, dates, and corrections.
ExtractabilityClear HTML, tables, definitions, and original diagrams.
